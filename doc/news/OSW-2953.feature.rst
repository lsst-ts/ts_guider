Integrated the streaming guider processor with CSC configuration,
DAQ acquisition, simulated stamp input, and enable/disable transitions.
Added the ``geometry_source`` CSC setting to select local guider tables
(the default), the installed LSST camera (``lsst``),
or the installed camera when available (``auto``).
The CSC logs the selected source before subscribing to DAQ stamps.
Added fault handling and deadlines for stopping acquisition
and draining queued publications.
Published acquisition metadata, camera-frame offsets in millimeters,
and per-sensor quality and shape moments through Guider events and telemetry.
Added diagnostic logs with combined offsets, uncertainties, scatter,
and contributing sensor counts.
